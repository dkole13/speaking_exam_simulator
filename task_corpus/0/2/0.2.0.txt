This is the campus gym.
Over there, you’ll see the weight machines and treadmills.
In the corner, there’s a room for yoga and fitness classes.
After you’ve finished using the equipment, please wipe it down.
Lockers are available near the entrance, if you want to store your bags.
The gym is open to students every day of the week.
Before leaving, don’t forget to return any towels you’ve borrowed.
